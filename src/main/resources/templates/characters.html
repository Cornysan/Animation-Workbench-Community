Where a figure comes from

Any humanoid .fbx or .glb. The file carries bone names but no mapping - a clip says LeftUpperArm, your skeleton may say mixamorig:LeftArm or upperarm_l. This page reads the names, works out the rest from how the skeleton hangs together, and shows you what it came up with. Correct anything it got wrong; it only has to be done once per figure.

Or straight out of the Workbench. Open the Figures view, and in its ⋯ menu pick Use my figures on the portal. Every humanoid character in the project turns into a card; click the ones that should stay home, then press Export and pick a folder. Those files bring their own mapping, so there is nothing to check - and a character built from parts, like a Synty Sidekick, can only come this way.

Two things to expect from a plain .fbx: it usually arrives grey, because FBX keeps its textures in files next to it rather than inside it, and it is measured in centimetres rather than metres - the page reads the unit out of the file and converts. What never travels either way: blend shapes, normal maps, LODs.
