Where a figure comes from

In the Animation Workbench: open the Figures view, and in its ⋯ menu pick Use my figures on the portal. Every humanoid character in the project turns into a card; click the ones that should stay home, then press Export and pick a folder. Drop what lands there on this page.

What goes into the file: the mesh, the skeleton, the skin weights, the base colour and the base texture, plus the measurements and the bone mapping this site needs to put a clip on it. What does not: blend shapes, normal maps, LODs.

Dropping a plain .fbx or a .glb from somewhere else is not in yet. Those files carry bone names but no mapping, and without it a clip cannot be put on them. Reading the names and asking you about the rest is the next step.
