Moderation

Reported clips are already hidden. Decide in your own time.

Switches

Off hides the Characters nav entry, the page itself and the figure row under every clip, and clips run on the mannequin again. Nothing is deleted: figures already added stay in that visitor's browser and come back when this is switched on.

Starter clips

Clips from a public CC0 collection, added under the Starter Clips account with their source on every clip page. Only for collections that really are CC0 – check the licence at the original source.

    Starter packs

    Clips from the same source belong together. A pack shows them as one card in the catalogue; search and tags still find each clip.

      Preview images

      The picture Discord, Slack and search results show for a link. Each public clip gets its own, rendered here with the same stage as the clip page; the owner's browser also renders it the next time they open their clip. Until then a clip shows the site image.
