Terms of use

Draft outline - to be written with legal review before launch (Plan L1). Not legal advice.

Points the terms must cover (Concept section 11)

  • Uploaders warrant they own the rights to what they share (the upload declaration) and indemnify the operator.
  • Grant of rights to the operator limited to the purpose: store, display, deliver, create previews.
  • Public clips are used under CC BY 4.0. A private clip grants no rights: having its link is not permission to use it.
  • Moderation: reported clips are hidden immediately; removal, strikes and suspension for repeat violations.
  • No marketplace, no payments.
  • Liability, availability, termination, applicable law.
