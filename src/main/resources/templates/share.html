Share your animations

Clips come into the Community from Unity. The Animation Workbench shares any humanoid clip in your project in two clicks - and brings the Community's clips into your project the same way.

  1. Get the Animation Workbench from the Unity Asset Store. The free edition shares, browses and imports without limits.
  2. Open the Community in the Workbench window and sign in - with Discord, GitHub or Google, the same account as here.
  3. Right-click a clip in the Clips view and choose Share with the Community… Public clips appear here: on the wall, on your profile, and for everyone who follows you. Several clips at once become a pack.

What you can share

  • Humanoid clips you made yourself - keyframed or captured.
  • Not clips from Asset Store packs, motion libraries, Mixamo, other games or other members - editing one does not make it yours. The Workbench checks where a clip comes from before it goes up and blocks the ones it recognises.
  • Public clips are there for everyone to use. Private ones stay yours alone, on the portal and in the Workbench.

The community rules say what belongs here, the Workbench documentation walks through the Community view step by step.

Without Unity?

Upload an .fbx or .glb straight from Blender, Maya or a motion capture tool. The page reads the skeleton, shows the animation on the mannequin, and the Workbench imports it like any other clip.
