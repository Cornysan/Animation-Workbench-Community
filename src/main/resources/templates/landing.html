Loading a clip…

Three steps, no export pipeline

  1. 1

    Animate in Unity

    Pose, key and layer a humanoid clip in the Animation Workbench - on your own character, in your own project.

  2. 2

    Share the motion

    One .awclip file leaves your project: curves and a preview skeleton. Your character, your mesh and your rig stay where they are.

  3. 3

    Import anywhere

    The clip lands on any humanoid avatar, because it was never bound to one. Unity retargets it the way it retargets every humanoid clip.
