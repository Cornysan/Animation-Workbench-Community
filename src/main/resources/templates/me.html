Closing your account

Closing it takes your name, your profile, your collections and your likes with it, and withdraws your clips from the catalogue. Comments stay without your name so the conversations under other people's clips stay readable, and copies of your clips that others already took remain theirs - that is what CC BY 4.0 means.
