No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no external fonts or scripts. Everything the page loads comes from this server.

Two cookies are set, both strictly necessary, both without an alternative: JSESSIONID keeps you signed in for the session, and XSRF-TOKEN stops a foreign page from acting in your name. Neither is used to recognise you anywhere else, which is why this site has no cookie banner. When you link the Workbench to your account, your browser briefly remembers the device code under aw-link-code in its own session storage; it never reaches the server and disappears when you close the tab.

How long

  • IP addresses in declarations, reports and takedowns: replaced by a keyed pseudonym after 30 days. The record stays, the address does not. Running this is a scheduled job on the server, not a promise for later.
  • Your account and uploads: until you delete them or the account.
  • Removed clips: the file goes, the record of the decision stays – it is what stops the same clip being uploaded again.
  • Audit log and moderation decisions: kept as long as they can still matter for a dispute.
  • Server logs: a few days, rotating.

Your rights

You may ask for access (Art. 15), correction (Art. 16), deletion (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), portability (Art. 20), and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). One email to the address above is enough; no form.
