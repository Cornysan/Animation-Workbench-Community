What you can upload

  • Humanoid animations you made yourself - keyframed or captured.
  • Not animations from Mixamo, Asset Store packs, motion libraries or games. A Mixamo rig is turned away here, as it is in the Workbench.
  • One animation per upload. A file with several lets you pick one.

The page names every bone of your skeleton the way Unity does - LeftUpperArm, Spine - and shows you what it came up with under the stage. If an arm or a leg moves wrong, fix the bones there before you upload. The rules for what belongs here are the community rules.
