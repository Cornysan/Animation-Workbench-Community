Community rules

Draft - review the wording before launch (Plan L1).
  1. Only share what you made. Clips from Asset Store packs, Mixamo, other games or other community members are not yours to share, even after editing them. The Workbench blocks the ones it can recognize.
  2. Public means public. Under CC BY 4.0 people may use and change your clip, also commercially. Pick private if you do not want that.
  3. Describe honestly. Title, tags and preview should match the motion.
  4. Report real problems only. Reports hide clips immediately; reports found to be unfounded count against your account.
  5. Repeat violations end in suspension. A removed clip counts as a strike; three strikes suspend the account.
