Draft outline - to be written with legal review before launch (Plan L1). Not legal advice.

What the portal stores - as built

  • Account: Discord user ID and display name (Discord scope identify, no email).
  • Uploads: the .awclip file, title, description, tags, license, and an upload declaration with its exact wording, time and IP address.
  • Reports and takedown requests: contents and time; for takedowns also name, email and IP address.
  • An audit log of uploads and moderation actions.
  • IP addresses are replaced by a keyed pseudonym after 30 days.
  • Workbench sign-ins: a token of which only a hash is stored.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no third-party scripts.
